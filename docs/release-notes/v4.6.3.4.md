## What's changed

**The RTC feature extensions ship.** Twelve new `Net.Agora.Extensions.<Name>.Android` packages
carry the native payloads Agora publishes separately from `full-rtc-basic`: AI noise suppression,
AI echo cancellation, audio beautification, spatial audio, virtual background, content inspection,
video enhancement, face capture, face detection, video quality analysis, and the software
H.264/AV1 encoders. Every one is a `.aar` with a 22-byte empty `classes.jar` and nothing but
`jni/<abi>/*.so`, so they bind nothing and depend on neither RTC package — see
[src/Agora.Extension.md](../../src/Agora.Extension.md), including why screen sharing is not among
them.

`Net.Agora.Video.Android` and `Net.Agora.Voice.Android` advance to **4.6.3.4** with them, from
revision 3, purely for this packaging train — their bound surface is identical to 4.6.3.3, since
the switches these extensions need were already generated. The bump keeps the whole RTC track on
one revision, matching `Net.Agora.Video.iOS` / `Voice.iOS` 4.6.2.4.
